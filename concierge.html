Concierge FAQ

Is the concierge really free?

Yes. Requesting a proposal costs nothing. If you order, you buy directly from the supplier you choose; suppliers may pay us a referral fee, which never changes your price.

What is the minimum order?

We handle requests of 10 gifts or more. For fewer gifts, the Gift Finder and Gift Ideas will get you there faster.

How fast can you deliver?

Proposals arrive within 48 hours (one business day for rush requests). Production lead times depend on the item and branding: typically one to three weeks for stock items and three to six weeks for custom branded products. Before Lunar New Year and Diwali, start at least six to eight weeks ahead.

Can you ship to multiple countries?

Yes. Many of our suppliers ship across Asia, North America and Europe, and some offer individual home delivery for remote teams.

How do you choose suppliers?

We review each supplier's product quality, reviews, response times and ability to handle branding and deadlines. See our supplier standards.
