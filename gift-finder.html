How the Gift Finder works

Each of our 60+ gift ideas is tagged by the cultures where it is traditional or well received, the occasions it suits, typical recipients, price band and style. The Finder scores every idea against your answers, ranks the best six, and then cross-checks the recipient's culture against our symbolism dictionary to list the items you should steer clear of.

It is a starting point, not a rulebook. Families differ, and people who have lived abroad for a long time may not mind traditional taboos at all. If you are unsure, an edible gift in a neat wrap is rarely wrong.

Is my answer data saved?

Only in your own browser, so the Finder can remember your last search and your saved list. Nothing is sent to us unless you submit a form.
