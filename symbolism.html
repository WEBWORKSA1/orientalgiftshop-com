Symbolism Dictionary

The colours, numbers, animals, flowers and objects that carry meaning in Asian gift giving, compared across six cultures.

How to use this dictionary

Hover or tap a culture badge to see the local nuance. Symbols are shared across Asia but often differ in meaning: red is joyful in China and India but never used for a living person's name in Korea; the number 9 means "long-lasting" in Chinese but sounds like "suffering" in Japanese. When a gift touches more than one symbol (a set of four white cups), the strongest warning wins. Check whole gift ideas with the Taboo Checker.
