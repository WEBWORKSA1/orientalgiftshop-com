Plan gifting backwards from the date

Shipping across borders before Lunar New Year and Diwali is slow because volumes spike and factories close for the holiday. Order international gifts four to six weeks ahead, domestic gifts two weeks ahead, and perishable gifts such as mooncakes or fruit boxes for delivery in the last week.
