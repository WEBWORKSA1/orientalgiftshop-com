Year of the Goat Gift Story Contest

Tell us about the most meaningful gift you have given or received across cultures, or share a photo of a beautifully wrapped gift. Win a US$100 gift card.

How to enter

  1. Write or photograph

    Up to 300 words about a gift that meant something, or a photo of a gift you wrapped (furoshiki, red envelope, Diwali hamper…).

  2. Submit the form

    Judged on story, creativity and cultural insight after the contest closes on 2027-02-20.
