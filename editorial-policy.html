Standards

Editorial policy

Accuracy

Every guide is based on multiple sources: etiquette guides from cultural organisations and tourism boards, academic and journalistic writing on gift customs, and the lived experience of people from each culture. Where customs differ by region, religion or generation, we say so rather than presenting one version as universal.

Review

Guides are reviewed by someone with first-hand knowledge of the culture before publication and re-checked at least once a year, and before the relevant festival.

Independence

Advertisers, sponsors and suppliers cannot buy a recommendation or a positive mention. Sponsored placements are always labelled "Sponsored". Affiliate links do not influence which products we suggest.

Respect

We write about cultures with respect and avoid stereotypes. We use "oriental" only in the historical sense of objects and shops, never to describe people, and we name cultures specifically.

Original content

All text, data and graphics on this site are original. We do not copy other publishers' articles, images or product photos. Embedded videos play from YouTube under YouTube's terms and remain the property of their creators.

Corrections

If you spot an error, tell us. Confirmed errors are corrected promptly and significant corrections are noted on the page.

Not professional advice

Our content is general cultural information, not legal, tax, religious or financial advice. For corporate gifts, check your own company's and the recipient's anti-bribery and gift policies.
