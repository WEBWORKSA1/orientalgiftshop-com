Why the birth date matters

The Chinese zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, which falls between January 21 and February 20, not on January 1. Someone born on January 20, 2027 is still a Horse (2026), while someone born on February 7, 2027 is a Goat. Most online charts get this wrong for January and February birthdays; our tool checks the exact New Year date for every year from 1930 to 2035.

Zodiac gifts done well

  • Give the coming year's animal at Lunar New Year. In 2027 that is the Goat: figurines, charms, red envelopes and snacks printed with the year's animal are fun and seasonal.
  • Personal sign gifts work for birthdays. A small jade or gold charm of someone's own animal is a classic.
  • Your own zodiac year (本命年) is considered challenging. Traditionally people wear red, often red underwear, socks or a red string bracelet given by family, for protection that year.

Zodiac traits and lucky colours are cultural folklore, shared for fun and gift inspiration.
