A new home is one of the most celebrated life events across Asia, and each culture has its own lucky gifts for it. Here are the favourites, and the mistakes to avoid.

## Chinese housewarming (入伙 / 乔迁)

Chinese housewarming gifts wish the household prosperity, harmony and abundance.

- **Lucky bamboo** in 2, 3, 6, 8 or 9 stalks (never 4)
- **A money plant or jade plant** for wealth
- **Fruit,** especially pineapples (in Hokkien and Cantonese, "pineapple" sounds like "prosperity arrives") and mandarins
- **Rice and salt,** traditionally the first items brought into a new home
- **Tea sets or a fruit bowl** for entertaining
- **A calligraphy scroll** with 福 (fortune) or 家和万事兴 (a harmonious family prospers)

Avoid clocks, mirrors (unless the host asks), sharp objects and anything white or black.

## Korean housewarming (집들이)

Koreans famously bring **toilet paper** and **laundry detergent**. The toilet roll wishes that everything "unrolls" smoothly; detergent wishes wealth that "bubbles up" like suds. Modern guests add good kitchen items, a plant or premium fruit. Avoid shoes and anything in fours.

## Japanese housewarming

Gifts are modest and practical: good sweets, quality towels, a tea set, tableware in odd-numbered sets, or a bottle of sake. Lucky cats (**maneki-neko**) are popular for new shops and offices. Avoid anything associated with fire (ashtrays, candles, red lamps) for a new home, as they can suggest a house fire.

## Indian housewarming (griha pravesh)

At a **griha pravesh** puja, guests bring:

- **Sweets** and dry fruits
- **Brass or silver items:** diyas, a puja thali, a kalash or idols of Ganesha or Lakshmi
- **Tulsi (holy basil) plant**
- **Home décor:** torans for the doorway, urlis, wall hangings
- **Kitchen essentials:** a pressure cooker or quality cookware

Avoid leather, alcohol and black items for traditional households.

## Vietnamese housewarming (tân gia)

Guests bring fruit, flowers, a plant, a framed painting or a practical item for the house. Lucky items include pictures of carp, horses or bamboo. Avoid sets of four and sharp objects.

## Universal safe choices

- A healthy plant (avoid potted plants for anyone in hospital, but they are perfect for homes)
- A tea set for entertaining, with two, six or eight cups
- A beautiful serving bowl or tray
- A gourmet hamper for the first weekend in the house
