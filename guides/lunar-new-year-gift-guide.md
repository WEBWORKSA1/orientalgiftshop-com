Lunar New Year 2027 begins on **Saturday, February 6, 2027**, opening the Year of the Fire Goat. It is celebrated as Spring Festival (春节) in China, Seollal in Korea, Tết in Vietnam and by Chinese communities from Singapore and Malaysia to Toronto and San Francisco. Celebrations traditionally run for fifteen days, ending with the Lantern Festival on February 20, 2027.

Gifts during this period do two jobs: they wish the recipient luck for the year ahead, and they honour relationships, especially with elders, in-laws, employers and clients. Here is how to get them right.

## The three gifts that matter most

**1. Red envelopes (hongbao, lai see, lì xì, sebaetdon).** Cash in a red envelope is the defining gift of the season. Married adults give them to children and unmarried younger relatives; working adults often give them to their parents; employers give them to staff. Use crisp new notes, avoid any amount containing a 4, and hand the envelope over with both hands along with a greeting. Our [Lucky Amount Calculator](lucky-amount.html) suggests suitable figures.

**2. A visiting gift (伴手礼).** When you visit family or friends during the New Year, never arrive empty-handed. The classic is a bag of **mandarin oranges, always in an even number**, because the Cantonese word for mandarin sounds like "gold" and the Mandarin word for tangerine (橘 jú) sounds like "luck" (吉 jí). Boxes of New Year cakes (nian gao), quality tea, pineapple tarts in Singapore and Malaysia, or a fruit basket all work well.

**3. Gifts for elders.** Health is the most meaningful wish for parents, grandparents and in-laws. Premium tea, bird's nest, ginseng, a quality scarf or a health-food hamper signal care. For Korean elders at Seollal, gift sets of fruit, beef, red ginseng or premium oils are traditional.

## Year of the Goat gift ideas

The Goat (羊, also translated as Sheep or Ram) is associated with gentleness, creativity and calm. 2027 is a Fire Goat year. Goat-themed gifts are playful and seasonal: plush toys for children, gold or jade charms, cashmere and wool (a gentle nod to the animal), art supplies or handmade ceramics for creative friends. People born in Goat years (for example 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003 and 2015, after that year's New Year date) are entering their own zodiac year, traditionally considered a challenging one, so a gift of **something red to wear**, such as socks, a scarf or a red string bracelet, is a thoughtful touch. ## What to avoid

- **Clocks and watches.** "Giving a clock" (送钟) sounds like attending a funeral (送终).
- **Scissors, knives and sharp objects.** They suggest cutting ties, and many families avoid using scissors on New Year's Day itself.
- **Pears.** "Pear" (梨) sounds like "separation" (离).
- **Anything in fours,** and amounts containing 4.
- **White or black wrapping,** both linked to mourning. Use red, gold or bright colours.
- **Books for business owners or mahjong players.** "Book" (书) sounds like "lose" (输).
- **Shoes,** which in some dialects sound like "evil" and suggest walking away.

## How to give

Present gifts with both hands and a greeting such as **新年快乐** (Xīnnián kuàilè, Happy New Year) or **恭喜发财** (Gōngxǐ fācái, wishing you prosperity). Recipients may politely decline once or twice before accepting; this is courtesy, not rejection. Don't expect the gift to be opened in front of you. When you leave, hosts often give you oranges back, an exchange of good luck.

## Timing and shipping

Factories and couriers across Asia slow down or close for one to two weeks around the holiday. Order international gifts by early January 2027, domestic gifts by late January, and perishable hampers for delivery in the first days of February. Businesses sending client gifts should lock in orders by mid-December.

## Lunar New Year for business

Client gifts are expected in many Chinese-speaking markets and appreciated everywhere. Choose consumables with premium packaging in red and gold, include a handwritten card, and avoid anything too personal.
