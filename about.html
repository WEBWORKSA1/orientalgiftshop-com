About

Gifts carry meaning. We help you get it right.

OrientalGiftShop.com is an independent guide to giving gifts across Asian cultures. We help people choose, wrap and present gifts for Lunar New Year, Diwali, Mid-Autumn, Chuseok, weddings, business meetings and everyday visits, and avoid the well-meant mistakes that come from not knowing a custom.

The idea is simple. Gift customs in China, Japan, Korea, India, Vietnam and Thailand are rich, practical and often very different from Western habits. A clock, a pair of shoes or a set of four can send the opposite message to the one you intended. Most of that knowledge lives in families and is hard to find in one place. We bring it together, explain the reasons behind it, and turn it into tools you can use in a minute.

What we are, and are not

We are an editorial website and a set of free tools. We are not a store and we do not sell, ship or hold stock. The name refers to the long tradition of "oriental gift shops" selling Asian tea, ceramics and crafts; we use the word "oriental" only for objects and shops and name each culture specifically everywhere else.

How we research

Guides are written from published etiquette sources, cultural institutions and first-hand experience, and are checked by reviewers from the culture concerned. We describe common practice, note where regions or generations differ, and correct errors quickly. Read our editorial policy.

How we are funded

The site is funded by advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, referral fees from suppliers in our Gift Concierge network, some affiliate links, and reader support. None of these decide what we recommend.
