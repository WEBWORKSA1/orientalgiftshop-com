Privacy policy

This policy explains what information OrientalGiftShop.com ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (Gift Concierge, contact, newsletter, contest, careers, supplier, advertising or support pledge), we receive the information you enter, plus the page you submitted from, the page you first landed on, the referring website and any campaign tags in the link. Forms are delivered by email through the third-party service FormSubmit. We use this information only to respond to you and provide what you asked for.

Gift Concierge requests: if you tick the consent box, we may share your brief and contact details with up to three vetted suppliers so they can quote. Suppliers may use them only to respond to your request.

Information stored in your browser

Our tools store your preferences, saved gift list and dismissed notices in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clear your browser data to remove it.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message powered by Google's certified consent management platform before personalised ads are served.

Embedded videos

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load only when you press play. YouTube's privacy policy then applies.

Fonts and hosting

Fonts load from Google Fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links may contain affiliate tracking. The retailer may set cookies to attribute a purchase. We do not receive your personal or payment information from retailers.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). Contests are open to adults only.

Retention and your rights

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to respond and for our records, normally no longer than 24 months. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or object to its use. To make a request, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
