Tips for writing the card

  • Write by hand if you can. Even a short greeting copied in the original script is noticed and appreciated.
  • Never use red ink for names. In Korea and parts of China, names in red are associated with the deceased.
  • Match formality to the relationship. Japanese and Korean have polite forms; the phrases here use the polite form, which is safe for anyone.
  • Elders love health wishes. 身体健康 (good health) or 福如东海 (boundless fortune) suit grandparents better than prosperity wishes.

Pronunciation guides are simplified for English speakers. Vietnamese and Thai are tonal; listen to a native speaker if you plan to say the greeting aloud.
